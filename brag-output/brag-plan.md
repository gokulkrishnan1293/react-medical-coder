# /brag plan: CLAIRE Home tour

**What it is:** CLAIRE Review Workbench is a review screen for coders working ED and E/M downcode reconsiderations. This video is about one feature: the **guided tour of the Home screen (dashboard)**.

**Who it's for:** coders opening CLAIRE for the first time who land on a dashboard full of numbers (31 to review, 22 accepted, 35 min…).

**What it does for them:** it walks through each part of the dashboard live, on the real screen. It dims everything else, outlines the part being explained, puts a card beside it, and switches to the Cases tab when that part comes up.

**What sets it apart:** it runs on the live screen, not on screenshots. It starts on your first visit, then comes back with `?` or the Tour button. The walkthrough has 11 steps and takes "about a minute" (the tour's own copy).

**Hook:** the real dashboard. The scrim drops over it and the "Your home screen" card rises, under the caption "Every number on Home, explained."

**Visual identity:** the app's own tokens. Paper `#fffffd`, ink `#17222d`, accent teal `#0a6b74`, scrim `rgba(12,22,32,.42)`. Fonts are Public Sans and IBM Plex Mono. The tour's real outline and cards are captured from the running app at 2×.

**Tone:** `polished` with a little bounce from the music. It's a serious tool for medical coders, so there are no jokes.

**Format:** landscape 1920×1080, 30fps, about 22.6s.

**Music:** Happy Beats / Business Moves vol. 9 (ende.app), about 114.8 BPM. Step changes land on beats 2.65, 5.28, 8.44, 10.54, 12.65, 15.28 and 17.38. The effects are soft UI clicks on each simulated click, mixed under the music.
